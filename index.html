About the Author:

A.C. Chen is a high school student and the author of To Hold a Flame.

Summary:

The world ended centuries ago. Now, a new spark is about to ignite a war that could end it again.

Calvin must solve a decades-old mystery of a stolen secret tech before war erupts. But as he digs through the secret histories, his own personal secrets are threatened to be uncovered. Andrew must survive the wilderness to return to his sister. But then he discovers a secret that dwarves even the mystery of the stolen tech. As the two boys struggle to solve their own mysteries, an old tragedy hidden in the shadows of history binds them together to a fate that neither could ever imagine.
